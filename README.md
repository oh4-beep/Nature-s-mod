# Nature's Mod

A Fabric mod for **Minecraft Java 26.2** that rebuilds nature into a wilder, more realistic and far more
explorable world:

- **The Overworld:** towering mountain ranges, rivers that run down from the peaks over waterfalls, misty plunge
  pools, gaping cave mouths, ancient giant trees, and trees that actually topple when you chop them.
- **The Ocean:** 71 new sea creatures, some peaceful and some very much not, sea floors covered in anemones, sea fans,
  starfish, urchins and giant clams, two diving suits that let you breathe and swim fast underwater, and **the
  Abyss**: a sea a thousand blocks deep beneath the ocean floor, with its own biomes, mutated horrors, a leviathan
  and a mission that ends in a boss fight. Twelve **guardian bosses** hunt you on the way down; at the bottom wait
  **55 kinds of forgotten ruins, vaults and wonders**, the richest places in Minecraft, with netherite, diamonds,
  mob heads, music discs, elytra, eight **lost weapons** and the **Abyssal Enchanting Table**, which doubles
  every enchantment on an item.
- **The Nether:** twice as tall, with vast caverns, floating islands and colossal pillars, and made to feel like Hell:
  brimstone, scorched rock, ash and walls of living flesh, skulls on pikes, the bones of titans, five new biomes and
  three new strongholds: Infernal Citadels, Bone Pits and Ritual Circles.

## What it changes

### Terrain
- **A taller world.** The Overworld goes up to **y = 575** (vanilla stops at 319) so mountains have room to grow.
- **Colossal mountains.** Mountain ranges rise three to five times higher above the lowlands than in vanilla, with
  summits up to y = 400-500 and sharper, jagged peaks that pierce the clouds, while foothills still roll gently and
  lowlands stay lowlands.
- **Larger landscapes.** Mountain ranges and river valleys are over twice as large, continents ~40% larger and biomes
  25% larger, so the land reads as broad, believable scenery instead of patchwork, and the giant mountains stay
  majestic instead of spiky.
- **Deeper oceans.**
- **Dramatic cave openings.** Natural cave entrances are larger and more common.
- **A real snow line.** Every peak above roughly y = 290 is capped with snow, with bare rock and ice on steep faces.
  High in the mountains moss, vines and pools give way to bare rock, and waterfalls freeze into icefalls.

### Water
- **Mountain rivers.** Rivers rise from springs high in the mountains and follow the land downhill for hundreds of
  blocks, carving gorges (and the occasional tunnel) through ridges, stepping down in cascades and plunging over
  waterfalls until they reach the sea or a lake.
- **Cliff waterfalls.** Streams run to the edge of cliffs and pour over into plunge pools. In freezing places they
  are frozen into icefalls instead.
- **Cascading pools.** Chains of natural terraced pools stepping down hillsides, each spilling into the next.
- **Alpine tarns.** Clear lakes cradled in high mountain hollows, ringed with gravel and boulders.
- **Glaciers.** Tongues of ice pour from snowy basins high on the great peaks down into the valleys below: snowfields
  up top, bare ice lower down, deep blue ice in the walls of the crevasses that crack open where the ice runs
  steep, rubble moraines along the flanks, and a meltwater lake at the snout.
- **Cenotes and sinkholes.** Collapsed shafts in jungles and savannas, opening into caverns with turquoise pools
  and vines trailing all the way down.

### Caves
- **Grand cave mouths.** Huge arched openings into hillsides: mossy floors near the light, vines and glow berries
  hanging from the arch, dripstone deeper inside, and sometimes a spring flowing out of the cave.

### Landmarks
- **Natural arches.** Banded terracotta arches in the badlands, sandstone arches in the desert, stone arches in the
  highlands and sea arches on rocky beaches.
- **Hoodoos.** Clusters of striped rock spires with heavy cap stones rising out of the badlands.
- **Sea stacks.** Weathered rock pillars standing in the surf off the coast, grass on their crowns.
- **Hanging gardens.** Curtains of vines spill down cliff faces in jungles, swamps and forests, with moss creeping
  over the brink.

### Forests
- **Giant trees.** Ancient oaks and dark oaks with flared roots and sprawling limbs, autumn giants in orange, red and
  yellow, golden birches, towering redwoods in old-growth taigas, weeping willows along swamps and riverbanks,
  bottle-trunked baobabs in the savanna, and bleached dead snags.
- **Fallen logs.** Long trunks of trees that fell years ago, lying beside their stumps under moss, mushrooms and
  shelf fungi.
- **Richer forest floors.** Leaf litter, bushes, ferns, boulders and fireflies near water.

### Trees that fall
- **Chop through the trunk and the tree topples.** Everything above the cut swings down around the stump and lands
  on the ground as a fallen tree, crown and all. Big trees need their whole trunk cut through, just like in reality,
  and the stump stays behind. Don't stand where it falls.
- Sneak while chopping to take a single log the vanilla way.
- **Lightning** can split a tree and knock it over, and during **thunderstorms** the wind occasionally brings down a
  tree near you.

### Atmosphere
- Waterfalls are wreathed in **mist and spray** at their foot and **roar** louder the higher they are.

## The Ocean

### 71 new sea creatures
Every sea, lake and river is alive. Each creature has its own model, texture, animation, sounds, loot and habitat,
and they behave like the animals they are:

| Where | Creatures |
| --- | --- |
| Rivers, lakes and swamps | rainbow and brown trout, northern pike, catfish, carp, perch, sturgeon, piranhas (jungles) |
| Warm reefs | clownfish, blue tang, butterflyfish, queen angelfish, parrotfish, lionfish, moray eel, barracuda, reef shark, seahorse, box jellyfish |
| Open ocean | herring, mackerel, bluefin tuna, swordfish, manta ray, flounder, ocean sunfish, hammerhead and great white sharks, moon and lion's mane jellies, giant grouper |
| Cold and frozen seas | arctic char, icefish, Greenland shark |
| Deep water and the Abyss | coelacanth, lanternfish, hatchetfish, anglerfish, viperfish, gulper eel, fangtooth, black dragonfish, barreleye, giant squid, vampire squid, dumbo octopus, tripod fish, goblin shark, frilled shark, comb jelly, ghost shark |
| The Abyss's mutants | rotfin, hollow eye, bone shark, twinhead eel, pale lurker, abyssal maw |
| The Leviathan's Lair | the **Abyssal Leviathan**, a boss |
| The Drowned Sanctum | the **Abyssal Horror**, the final boss of the Abyss |
| The descent through the Abyss | the twelve **guardians**: Tidewyrm, Razorjaw, Gloomlantern, Stormcoil, the Kraken, the Siren Queen, the Bonewarden, the Abyssal Manta, the Hollow King, Dunkleosteus, the Twin Terror and the Megalodon |

- **Peaceful fish** dart about, flee from danger and can be scooped up in a **bucket**. Schooling fish move in
  shoals that scatter when something big comes near.
- **Hunters** (barracudas, sharks, the deep-sea predators) hunt fish and attack divers in the water, and sharks
  **smell blood**: a wounded swimmer is noticed from much further away.
- **Ambush predators** (moray eels, anglerfish) lurk motionless in their holes and lunge at anything that comes
  too close. **Piranhas** swarm whatever bleeds in their river.
- **Neutral giants** (pike, swordfish, Greenland sharks, groupers) leave you alone until you attack them.
- **Jellyfish** drift and pulse along; most of them **sting** with poison, the box jellyfish worst of all.
  Lionfish have venomous spines.
- Deep-sea creatures **glow**: photophores, lures, luminous stripes and eyes shine in the dark.
- The **Abyssal Leviathan** is a 20-block serpent with a boss bar, a roar that plunges divers into darkness, and
  charges that send them flying. It guards a hoard of treasure.
- The **mutants** of the Abyss are rotting, many-eyed, rib-cage things, and their bites curse you with darkness,
  wither, blindness, poison or nausea.
- Creatures drop **fish fillets** (cook them for a good meal), **sea scales**, **shark teeth** and, in the deep,
  **abyssal scales**.
- **The seas are crowded.** Fish keep arriving around you in every ocean, reef, river and in the Abyss (far more
  than vanilla's mob caps would allow); turn it up or down with `seaLifeDensity`.

### Life on the sea floor
Warm reefs grow **red, purple and green sea anemones** (they glow faintly), **purple and orange sea fans**, orange
and blue **starfish**, **seashells**, **sea urchins** and **giant clams**, alongside far more coral, kelp, seagrass and
sea pickles than vanilla. Temperate and cold seas have their own mix, and beaches are strewn with shells.

- **Sea urchins** sting and poison whoever treads on them (sea creatures know better).
- **Giant clams** sometimes hold a **pearl**: use a clam to take it, and in water it slowly grows a new one. Nine
  pearls make a glowing **pearl block**.

### Diving suits
| Suit | Made from | Every piece | Full suit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tidewalker** | 24 sea scales | longer breath (helmet, chestplate), faster swimming (leggings, boots), faster mining underwater (helmet) | water breathing + dolphin's grace |
| **Abyssal** | Tidewalker gear + abyssal plate + Abyssal Upgrade template at a smithing table | much more of the same, diamond-grade protection | water breathing + dolphin's grace + conduit power |

### The Abyss
**Abyssal Rifts** tear open the floor of the deep oceans: a funnel ringed by ancient prismarine pillars, leading
down to a swirling portal. Treasure maps to the nearest rift turn up in shipwrecks, ocean ruins and buried treasure.
Swim into a rift and you surface in the Abyss, on a small platform floating on a black, starless sea, next to a
rift that takes you home. Below you the water goes down for **a thousand blocks**:

- **Twilight Waters** (above y 730), the last of the light.
- **The Midnight Zone**: black open water around the flanks of seamounts up to 500 blocks tall.
- **The sea floor** (y 60-110): **Abyssal Plains** of grey ooze, **Glowing Reefs** of luminous coral trees,
  **Hydrothermal Vents** with black smokers and bubbling soul vents, and **Bone Fields** strewn with the skeletons of
  whales. Trenches cut down to bedrock and lonely rock spires tower out of the plains. Deepslate ores everywhere.
- **Pressure.** The deeper you dive, the harder the water presses on you. Each Tidewalker piece is worth 1 point of
  pressure rating and each Abyssal piece 2: below y 700 you need 4 (a full Tidewalker suit), below y 350 you need 8
  (a full Abyssal suit), and for every point short the pressure hurts you half a heart a second. Conduit power
  shields you completely. The first Abyssal Upgrade template can be found in rift shrines and bathysphere wrecks.
- **It is dark, and it is not empty.** You can see a good way through the water, but the fog closes in beyond that.
  Piles of skulls and bones, the remains of drowned divers slumped beside their chests, giant kelp forests, glowing
  growths hanging under overhangs, and something roaring, far off, now and then.
- **Structures:** **Sunken Temples** (treasure, the Abyssal Upgrade template, and a rift home in their courtyard),
  **Bathysphere** wrecks with dry cabins and their crew's belongings, the **Leviathan's Lair**, a bowl under a
  colossal ribcage where the Abyssal Leviathan waits on its hoard, **Vent Shrines** of blackstone among the
  hydrothermal vents, and the **Drowned Sanctums**, vast ruined cathedrals on the deep floor.

### The descent: twelve guardians
Getting to the bottom of the Abyss is the challenge. The water from y 1000 down to y 160 is split into twelve bands,
each kept by a **guardian boss**, and when you dive into a guardian's band it comes out of the dark to hunt you (one
at a time, with a boss bar and a warning). Every guardian has its own powers on top of its charge:

| Guardian | Health | Its powers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tidewyrm (y 1000-930) | 160 | whirlpools that drag you in, nausea |
| Razorjaw (930-860) | 180 | blood frenzy: three charges in a row, wither |
| Gloomlantern (860-790) | 200 | a blinding lure flash, blindness |
| Stormcoil (790-720) | 210 | lightning discharges |
| The Kraken (720-650) | 240 | tentacle grabs that pull you in, ink clouds |
| The Siren Queen (650-580) | 220 | poison blooms, broods of lion's mane jellies |
| The Bonewarden (580-510) | 260 | barrages of wither skulls, bone sharks |
| The Abyssal Manta (510-440) | 230 | dives into invisibility and strikes from nowhere |
| The Hollow King (440-370) | 280 | a crushing gaze, hollow eyes at its side |
| Dunkleosteus (370-300) | 320 | pressure waves, crushing bites |
| The Twin Terror (300-230) | 300 | venom spit from two heads |
| The Megalodon (230-160) | 400 | frenzies, and it gets faster when it is hurt |

Slay a guardian and its depth is safe for you for 20 minutes (`bossRespawnMinutes`); escape it and it comes back in 3.
Guardians drop Abyssal Hearts, diamonds, netherite scrap and sometimes one of the lost weapons.

### The bottom: forgotten riches
Below the last guardian (y 150 and down) the floor of the Abyss is the **richest place in Minecraft**. Its rock is
thick with diamond ore and **ancient debris**, much of it right at the surface, and every hundred blocks or so stands
one of **55 kinds of structures** left by a drowned people, every one lit, looted with treasure and different:

- **20 ruins**: column circles, a fallen colossus with a golden crown, treasure piles of gold, coral gazebos, a well
  with something at the bottom, skull shrines, a hermit's cell, spilled mine carts, a giant anchor, a diving bell,
  bone altars, obsidian monoliths ringed with ancient debris, cracked amethyst geodes full of diamonds, tombs, ruined
  arches, a pearl bed with a giant clam, a fallen lighthouse, a stone head, a lost explorer's camp and debris veins.
- **20 vaults**: drowned houses, an ancient library, an armory with weapon racks and suits of armor, a treasury of
  gold and netherite, a music hall with records on the walls, a museum of mob heads (dragon head included), a sunken
  End ship with **elytra** in its cabin, a forge, an enchanting sanctum, a throne room, a treasure galleon, a copper
  submarine, a crypt, a pyramid, a coral palace, an observatory, a prison, an arena, a sea dragon's skeleton on its
  hoard and a sunken market.
- **15 wonders**: a drowned cathedral, a ziggurat crowned with a working **beacon**, a walled citadel, a 40-block
  colossus, a quarter of a drowned city, a titan's skull, the **Great Vault**, the **Temple of the Deep** with a
  working **conduit**, a leviathan graveyard, the Abyssal Gate, a coral tower forty blocks high, a sea dragon's nest,
  a mining outpost in an open pit of ore, a laboratory of glass domes and a royal tomb. Some are still guarded by a
  boss of the deep.

Their chests hold diamonds, netherite, gold and emerald blocks, mob heads, music discs, armor trims, elytra, totems,
enchanted golden apples, enchanted books at their **highest levels**, and the two prizes found nowhere else:

- **The lost weapons**, each with a strike effect and a power on use: the **Tidecleaver** (a tidal wave), the
  **Leviathan Fang** (shadowstep behind your target), the **Abyssal Scythe** (a reaping sweep that heals you), the
  **Stormcaller** (lightning), **Kraken's Grasp** (pull a foe to you), the **Pressure Maul** (a crushing shockwave),
  the **Sovereign's Staff** (a line of snapping jaws) and the **Coral Rapier** (a lunge). Better than netherite,
  repaired with abyssal plate, and they can only be found, never crafted.
- **The Abyssal Enchanting Table**: use an enchanted tool, weapon or piece of armor on it and **every enchantment is
  doubled**: Fortune III becomes Fortune VI, Sharpness V becomes Sharpness X. Each item can be empowered only
  once. Found in libraries, sanctums, laboratories, the Great Vault and the Temple of the Deep, rarely in chests, or
  crafted from an Abyssal Heart, two diamond blocks, an enchanting table and three netherite ingots.

### The Drowned Expedition
The first time you reach the Abyss, the **logbook** of the expedition that came before you drifts down beside you.
It tells you what they were after:

1. Find the four **sigils** that seal the Abyss: the **Sigil of the Tide** in the Sunken Temples, the **Sigil of the
   Deep** in the bathysphere wrecks, the **Sigil of Bone** inside the Abyssal Leviathan, and the **Sigil of the
   Ember** in the Vent Shrines.
2. Find a **Drowned Sanctum** (the temple chests hold maps to them) and set all four sigils into its **Abyssal Altar**.
3. Survive what wakes: the **Abyssal Horror**, 500 health of rot and teeth. It drops the **Crown of the Deep**, which
   makes its wearer immune to the pressure of the Abyss and gives water breathing, conduit power, dolphin's grace
   and night vision underwater.

## The Nether

- **Twice as tall** (256 blocks). The classic Nether with its lava sea fills the lower half, so fortresses and
  bastions still have their place; above it open **vast caverns** where **islands of netherrack float** in the air
  and **colossal pillars** hold up the roof.
- **Ember Wastes**: ash, blackstone and rivers of magma, fields of **erupting geysers**, blazes roaming in the open.
- **Glowstone Grotto** (upper caverns): giant glowstone chandeliers hanging over teal and crimson moss; piglins
  hunting for gold.
- **Obsidian Spires** (upper caverns): a forest of weeping obsidian spires crowned with soul fire; wither skeletons
  and ghasts.
- **Lavafalls** pour from cliffs and cavern roofs everywhere, and **iron cages** dangle on chains over the drops,
  some with treasure left by their last prisoner.
- **Nether geysers** rumble when stepped on and a moment later blast whatever stands on them into the air in a column
  of fire. They erupt on their own now and then, too.

### Hell
- **Brimstone Fields**: smouldering brimstone with sulfur and cinnabar breaking through it, fires that never go out,
  glowing **ember crystals**, geysers, and blazes drifting overhead.
- **Flesh Caverns**: walls of living flesh blistered with glowing boils and dripping blood-red vines, the skeletons
  of titans with ribs arching overhead, fields of **bone spikes** and skulls on pikes.
- **The old biomes are reworked too**: scorched, glowing netherrack and drifts of ash on the ground of the Nether
  Wastes, Soul Sand Valleys and Basalt Deltas; flesh creeping through the roof of the Crimson Forests; embers and soot
  in the air; burnt-out **charred trees**, **skulls on pikes** (now and then with someone still on one), small ruined
  **shrines** with burning braziers, brimstone and ember crystals; more blazes and wither skeletons roaming in the
  open; and screams, far off in the dark.
- **Infernal Citadels**: fortresses of scorched brick and blackstone behind a moat of lava, with corner towers, a
  road of braziers to a great keep, a throne between two treasure chests, wither skeletons rising from the hall
  floor and blazes on the roof.
- **Bone Pits**: great pits lined with bone and soul soil, giant ribs curling over the edge, soul fire on the floor
  and a chest on a mound of bones guarded by wither skeletons.
- **Ritual Circles**: raised circles of black stone inlaid with blood-red brick, ringed by obsidian pillars crowned
  with braziers, with an offering on the altar and caged skulls watching.

## Advancements

A new **Deep Sea** advancement tab leads from your first catch to the Abyss: bucket a fish, earn a shark tooth, wear
a full Tidewalker suit, swim through a rift, dive below y 350, find a Sunken Temple, wear a full Abyssal suit, slay the
Abyssal Leviathan, and then the Drowned Expedition: find a sigil, hold all four, wake the Abyss, defeat the Abyssal
Horror and wear its crown; and the descent: slay a guardian, slay all twelve, reach the bottom, find the forgotten
riches, stand in a wonder of the deep and get the Abyssal Enchanting Table. The Nether tab gains advancements for climbing into the upper caverns, for finding each new
biome and for each of the three strongholds.

## Installing

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) 0.18.0 or newer for Minecraft 26.2.
2. Put [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) and the Nature's Mod jar into your `mods` folder.
3. Create a **new world**. Existing worlds keep their old terrain; only newly generated chunks use the new terrain,
   which leaves visible seams. This goes for the Nether too.

The mod is needed on both the server and the client.

## Configuration

`config/naturesmod.json` is created on first launch:

| Option | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `treeFelling` | `true` | Chopping through a natural tree's trunk makes it topple. |
| `fellingRequiresAxe` | `true` | Only fell trees when using an axe. |
| `sneakToChopNormally` | `true` | Sneak to break a single log. |
| `fellInCreative` | `false` | Also fell trees in creative mode. |
| `fallingTreesHurt` | `true` | Falling logs hurt whatever they land on. |
| `logsPerExtraDurability` | `8` | Extra axe wear: one durability per this many felled logs (0 = none). |
| `maxTreeLogs` | `2500` | Largest tree, in logs, that can be felled at once. |
| `lightningFellsTrees` | `true` | Lightning strikes can fell trees. |
| `stormWindthrow` | `true` | Thunderstorms occasionally blow over trees near players. |
| `windthrowMinutes` | `6` | Average minutes of thunderstorm between wind-thrown trees per player. |
| `abyssPressure` | `true` | Diving deep in the Abyss without a strong enough diving suit hurts. |
| `hostileSeaCreatures` | `true` | Sharks, eels and the other hunters attack divers. |
| `abyssBosses` | `true` | The twelve guardians hunt divers on the way down through the Abyss. |
| `bossRespawnMinutes` | `20` | How long a slain guardian leaves you alone before it can hunt you again. |
| `seaLifeDensity` | `1.0` | How crowded the seas are kept around players (0 = only vanilla spawning). |
| `waterfallMist` | `true` | Mist and spray at the foot of waterfalls. |
| `mistDensity` | `1.0` | Amount of waterfall mist. |
| `waterfallSounds` | `true` | Waterfalls roar. |

Only natural trees fall: a group of logs must carry natural (non-player-placed) leaves, so log cabins and other
builds are never knocked over.

## Building

Requires JDK 25.

```sh
./gradlew build          # the mod jar ends up in build/libs/
./gradlew runClient      # play with the mod in a development client
./gradlew runSmokeTest   # headless world generation check that prints a terrain report
./gradlew runClientGameTest  # plays through the new content in a real client and takes screenshots
```

The smoke test boots a dedicated server, generates the spawn area plus the nearest example of a dozen landmark
biomes (peaks, jungle, badlands...), prints statistics, ASCII relief maps and feature counts, renders a top-down
map and a bird's-eye view of every region to `run/smoketest/maps/`, fells a tree, then surveys and renders the
Abyss and the Nether, finds and checks every new structure, builds all 55 treasures of the deep and opens their
chests, rides an Abyssal Rift down and back, and lets all 71 sea creatures swim, die and drop their loot. CI runs it on every push (see `.github/workflows/build.yml`), along with
the client game test. It needs an accepted `eula.txt` in `run/smoketest/`.

### World generation data

The terrain and feature JSON in `src/main/resources/data` is generated by `tools/worldgen/generate.py` from the
vanilla data pack of Minecraft 26.3, in 26.3's formats, and `tools/port/to262.py` then rewrites it into the formats
of 26.2, which the mod targets (see the script for what changes and how it was checked):

```sh
git clone --depth 1 --branch 26.3-data https://github.com/misode/mcmeta vanilla-26.3
git clone --depth 1 --branch 26.2-data https://github.com/misode/mcmeta vanilla-26.2
python3 tools/worldgen/generate.py --vanilla vanilla-26.3/data
python3 tools/port/to262.py --vanilla vanilla-26.2/data --vanilla-from vanilla-26.3/data
```

Tweak the terrain in `tools/worldgen/terrain.py` (world height, mountain stretch, biome scale, cave entrances, snow
line) and the features in `tools/worldgen/features.py`, the Abyss in `tools/worldgen/abyss.py` and the Nether in
`tools/worldgen/nether.py`, then re-run the script. Which Overworld biomes get which feature is set in
`ModBiomeModifications.java`.

### Sea creatures

The 52 creatures are generated by `tools/creatures/generate.py` from `tools/creatures/species.py`: each species
picks a body plan (fish, deep-bodied fish, eel, shark, ray, jellyfish, squid, seahorse), colours, a pattern and
glowing markings, and the script writes its model code, textures, items, loot table and language entries:

```sh
python3 tools/creatures/generate.py
python3 tools/assets/generate.py   # block textures and names of the Abyss and the Nether
```

These write their loot tables and other data in 26.3's formats too, so run `tools/port/to262.py` after them as well.
